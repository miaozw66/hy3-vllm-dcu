Key Observations
================================================================================

1. Attention precision is excellent (02_attention_out: 0.998494 mean),
   confirming that BF16 dequantization + PyTorch SDPA with half-split RoPE
   matches MetaInfer's attention implementation.

2. MoE output (05_mlp_out: 0.993699 mean) has the largest deviation, which
   is expected due to accumulated numerical differences in:
   - Sigmoid routing with expert_bias
   - topk selection + renormalize
   - Weighted summation over 8 routed experts + shared expert
   - Per-expert SwiGLU activation (gate_proj/up_proj split + sigmoid gating)

3. Layer 67 shows the largest single-point deviation (05_mlp_out: 0.974602).
   This is likely a routing divergence where a borderline expert selection
   differs between our computation and MetaInfer due to float rounding.

4. Residual propagation is stable across all 15 manual layers:
   06_output stays above 0.997, confirming no error accumulation in the
   residual stream.

5. Overall 80-layer weighted mean (0.998553) confirms the vLLM inference
   pipeline is faithfully reproducing MetaInfer results.
